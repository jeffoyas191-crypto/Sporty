How it works

1. Strength ratings. Attack and defence ratings come from goals per game relative to the league average. Ratings from small samples are shrunk toward 1.0 so one freak result can't dominate.

2. Expected goals. λhome = home attack × away defence × league home avg (and the mirror for the away side).

3. Score matrix. Independent Poisson scorelines 0–10, corrected with the Dixon-Coles τ factor, which fixes Poisson's under-estimate of 0-0 and 1-1 draws.

4. Market blend. The bookmaker margin is removed from the odds and blended with the model. Closing odds are among the most accurate predictors there are, so ignoring them would make the model worse.

5. Value & stake. Edge = probability × odds − 1. A positive edge is a "value" bet. Stake uses quarter-Kelly and is capped at 5% of your bankroll.
